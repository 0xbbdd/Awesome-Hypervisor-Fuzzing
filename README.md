# Awesome Hypervisor Fuzzing

A curated list of papers on **hypervisor fuzzing** — fuzzing hypervisors / virtual machine monitors (QEMU, KVM, Xen, Hyper-V, ...) and the **virtual devices** (device models) they emulate for guest VMs. This umbrella covers virtual device fuzzing, virtual CPU (vCPU) fuzzing and nested-virtualization fuzzing.

_Formerly known as `Awesome-Virtual-Device-Fuzzing`._

- **Scope (main table)**: fuzzing hypervisors and their virtual devices / device models (QEMU, KVM, Xen, Hyper-V, ...), including virtual-CPU (vCPU) fuzzing and nested-virtualization fuzzing.
- **Target**: the hypervisors / device models actually evaluated in the paper (e.g. QEMU, KVM, Xen, Hyper-V, VirtualBox, bhyve, VMware, ...). `—` means the target is not publicly stated or the paper is behind a paywall.
- **Related work**: guest/driver-side bug hunting that *uses* virtual devices as an input channel (no device-model target), kept separately below so the two directions are not mixed.
- Sorted by year (newest first). Contributions are welcome — feel free to open a PR to add a paper, artifact, slide or missing link.

_Last updated: 2026-10-23._

## Notes

- **HyperFuzzer** (CCS'21) is Microsoft's internal Hyper-V fuzzer; only the hardware-independent fuzzing seeds are released (the fuzzer binary itself is not public).
- **Morphuzz** ships as modifications to QEMU rather than a standalone repository; the linked GitLab tree is the pinned QEMU revision used for the paper.
- **VD-GUARD** (ASE'23) — the paper is behind IEEE Xplore; the linked talk video is public.
- Slides / videos are only listed when they are publicly available; many venues (e.g. ICSE, EuroSys, journals) do not publish them.
